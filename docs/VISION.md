# Vision

## What this product is
FDD Tracker is a SaaS product for monitoring Franchise Disclosure Documents and surfacing meaningful changes so users can review risk and change faster.

## Who it serves
- franchise buyers evaluating opportunities
- franchise brokers and consultants comparing systems
- operators and analysts who need faster document-change visibility than manual review provides

## Core promise
FDD Tracker helps users detect and understand meaningful FDD changes faster, with less manual review burden and better operational consistency.

## Current constraints
- legal/operational nuance around sourcing and interpretation still matters alongside product capability
- trust depends on alert reliability, explainability, and observability
- go-to-market claims must stay grounded in what the monitored workflow actually supports

## Non-goals
- becoming a generic PDF tool without franchise-specific value
- overpromising legal/compliance guarantees beyond the actual monitored workflow
- letting product messaging outrun the verified monitoring pipeline

## What success looks like
- one end-to-end monitored flow is reliable and inspectable
- users can see meaningful diffs and alerts they actually trust
- the free tool and SaaS path reinforce each other clearly
- the product becomes a credible monitored SaaS, not just a sharp idea
